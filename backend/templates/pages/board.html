The Documentary

A journey built layer by layer.

Like a great coating system, a great career is applied one deliberate layer at a time — surface prepared, each coat inspected, nothing left to chance. This is the story of how a campus-selected Diploma Engineer from Surat became one of India's most trusted names in industrial surface treatment.

Career Objective — in his own words

“With over twenty years of surface treatment and corrosion experience, I want to apply my skills and knowledge of effective surface treatment to lead my team and exceed every goal — developing my leadership while supporting my people with everything I know about coating trends.”

01 · Learning — 2004

The first coat

Campus-selected into Larsen & Toubro Heavy Engineering, Hazira, as a Diploma Engineer Trainee — starting on the fabrication shop floor, supervising shifts on HDS reactors for Fluor and EO reactors for KBR.

02 · Engineering — 2006

Mastering the craft

Moving into coating, heat treatment and scaffolding, he turns theory into instinct — hands-on with epoxies, zinc, PU and MIO, managing 90+ workmen while learning that in pressure vessels, "good enough" does not exist.

03 · Credentials — 2009–2011

Earning the badges

BGAS-CSWIP Painting Inspector Grade 2, NACE CIP Level I, then NACE CIP Level II (No. 20438) — joining a select global community of certified inspectors, while introducing Thermal Spray Aluminium at L&T Hazira.

04 · Leadership — 2014–2020

Heading the department

As Head of Surface Treatment & Coating, he owns the lifecycle end to end — estimation, procedures, planning, execution, inspection — delivering ~140 equipment a year and mentoring the team beneath him.

05 · Innovation — 2015–2023

Pushing the frontier

Fireproofing introduced to Heavy Engineering. FRP linings for Hassiyan, Dubai. Peel-off preservation coatings. Water-based ceramic coating. Benchmark records in acid cleaning. The ITER nuclear fusion project's pickling and ultrasonic cleaning of 60,000+ components.

06 · Global Exposure

Crossing borders

Russia (2014 & 2016) to rescue erected VCC reactors for TOYO/TAIF-NK. Malaysia (2018) for KBR/LOTTE's K-COT converter. Dubai (2020) for GE's Hassiyan water-box lining. Every trip, a coating problem solved on foreign soil.

07 · Digital Transformation — 2018–2023

The digital era

As Coating Technical Expert leading ~200 equipment a year and 400 contract workmen, he builds online surface treatment systems, quality modules, dashboards — and pioneers AI-assisted coating estimation delivering 90% right-first-time inputs.

08 · Entrepreneurship — 2023

Building BG Group

After 18+ years at L&T, he founds BG Group of Paint & Coating Consultant — NACE inspection, client representation and consulting for Technip Energies, Praj, Afcons, Zeeco, Heatex and more, on projects from INEOS to BP NZT UK and LNG Canada.

09 · Mastery — 2025

Never stop learning

A Post Graduate Program in Corrosion Management & Technology from IIT Ropar caps two decades of practice with formal mastery — and sets the stage for the next chapter: digitising the coating industry itself.

The Craft — End-to-End Ownership

From tender document to final protocol.

Across three business units — Refinery & Petrochemical, Heat Transfer, and Nuclear — he owned every link of the surface treatment chain. This is what that actually means.

1 · Coating Estimation

Study the tender and every governing specification; raise technical queries at the earliest stage; work out the coating system from spec, operating parameters, preservation and transportation needs; cost each system per sq.m of raw material and labour — including acid cleaning — and carry the cost break-up and feasibility for every single equipment to management approval.

2 · Specification, Procedure & ITP

Work with Design and project teams on vessel parameters; take paint-manufacturer inputs on systems and performance; embed every testing parameter into the procedure; walk the client through procedure and ITP in pre-job and pre-inspection meetings; record and close every MOM point alongside document approval.

3 · Planning & Full-Kitting

Six-month advance departmental planning across RPV, HTE and Nuclear BUs; convert project lists into sq.m and order paint and abrasive; secure dehumidified sheds, blasting slots and priorities; and take extra care with the special processes — fireproofing, TSA/Zn, FRP, shot peening, glass flake lining, and acid or ultrasonic cleaning.

4 · Execution & Inspection

Get the work done through subcontractors while coordinating TPIs and clients — LRIS, BV, EIL, DNV; enforce British, SSPC, NACE and ISO standards; run every abrasive from shot and garnet to glass beads, and every system from inorganic zinc silicate to solvent-free epoxies by International, Jotun, Carboline, PPG, Hempel and Sherwin-Williams; monitor weather before and during application; execute pickling & passivation; own permits, protocols and timely handover.

5 · Digital & Change Management

Post-2018, structured data runs the department: online surface-treatment requests with finance and quantity approvals, quality modules quantifying contractor rejection rates, scaffolding dashboards, and a live vendor-rating system. AI estimation reached 90% right-first-time inputs, with non-contact DFT gauges, motion-sensor deadman switches and laser cleaning in the pipeline. And when responsibility itself changed — centralising purchase, subcontracting and stores in 2014, restructuring into Technical, Execution and Payment verticals in 2019 — he led the change rather than resisting it.
