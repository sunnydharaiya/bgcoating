Surface Treatment In Action

From Corrosion To Protection

Drag the slider to watch corroded steel transform under a full protective coating system — then follow the exact five-stage process our consultants control on every project.

BEFORE · RUSTED STEEL AFTER · COATED & PROTECTED

  1. Survey & Corrosion Assessment

    Condition mapping, rust grading and salt-contamination testing turn surface observations into corrosion intelligence.

  2. Surface Preparation · SA 2½

    Abrasive blasting to specification with anchor-profile (Rz) verification — the foundation every coating system depends on.

  3. Priming & Intermediate Coats

    Zinc-rich primer and epoxy intermediate layers applied under controlled climate, with wet-film checks at every pass.

  4. Topcoat Application

    Atomized spray application of the finish coat — colour, gloss and DFT built up layer by layer to the specified system.

  5. Inspection & Certification

    DFT verification, adhesion and holiday testing, then an auto-generated digital report — signed off to ISO, NACE, AMPP and SSPC standards.
